Sea shipping to Moldova

The estimated sea-shipping transit time is 21–35 days. Actual transit may vary with dispatch schedules, cargo consolidation, customs processing and delivery within the destination country. Before booking, our team will confirm the current route and estimated timing for your address.

Sea freight may suit senders who can plan ahead and want to consider shipment weight and volume alongside timing. The final price is calculated individually after the parcel details are reviewed.

Destinations and address coverage

Service may be available to major cities and other locations, including Chisinau, Balti, Orhei, Cahul, Ungheni and other destinations, subject to route availability. This list is illustrative; availability for a particular address must be checked before shipping.

Moldova can be a practical destination for personal parcels to relatives and friends. Prepare the recipient’s postal code, address and phone number, and confirm whether delivery is available to the door or through a collection point.

How to arrange a shipment from Israel

  1. Contact Admiral Post and provide the destination country and city.
  2. Share the approximate weight, box dimensions and a detailed contents list.
  3. Check whether the items are accepted and what packing or paperwork is required.
  4. Receive a quote and confirmation of route, delivery or collection method and estimated transit time.
  5. Hand over the parcel as agreed and keep the shipment reference if one is issued.

The delivery method and availability for a specific address should be confirmed when requesting a shipment. Please agree on the route and final price before handing over the parcel.

What affects the shipping price?

The price may depend on actual and volumetric weight, package dimensions, item type, destination country and address, additional services and current carrier conditions. Providing accurate box measurements helps us prepare a more useful estimate.

Customs and restricted items

Declare the actual contents, quantities and purpose of the items. If you are sending new goods, electronics or higher-value items, ask in advance which details or documents may be needed.

Do not pack potentially prohibited or regulated goods until our team confirms they can be carried. Requirements may change, so the contents should be approved before each shipment.

What to confirm before dispatch

  • Destination availability and the next dispatch date.
  • Estimated price based on weight and dimensions.
  • Contents declaration and document requirements.
  • Delivery or collection method and recipient contact details.
  • Estimated sea-shipping time of 21–35 days.

Frequently asked questions

How long does sea shipping take?

The general estimate is 21–35 days. Actual timing depends on the route, dispatch schedule, customs processing and delivery within the destination country.

How much does shipping cost?

Pricing is calculated individually based on weight, dimensions, contents, destination address and any additional services. Contact us for an estimate.

What information is needed for a quote?

For a quote, provide the destination city, approximate weight, box dimensions and item list. Our team will check the available shipping option and price.

What can I pack?

Acceptance depends on the item type, carrier rules and destination-country requirements. Confirm the contents with our team before packing.

How should I write the recipient address?

Provide the recipient’s full name, country, city, street, building and apartment number if applicable, postal code and current phone number. Additional details may be required for some destinations.

Is 21–35 days a guaranteed delivery time?

No. This is an estimated sea-shipping transit range, not a guarantee of arrival by a specific date. Our team will check the current estimate for your shipment.
